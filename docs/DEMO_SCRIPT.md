# Demo script (2 minutes)

From SPEC.md section 9.

**Scenario.** Mission days 1–30 on a deep-space transit, one astronaut. Days 1–12 are stable. From day 13, sleep drops and stress rises. On day 17 `LOOP-SLEEP-STRESS` fires. On day 20 resting heart rate rises and `LOOP-STRAIN-HEART` fires. The coach suggests a plan, and adherence on days 21–24 brings stress and sleep back toward baseline. A separate day shows the escalation path to the flight surgeon with the delay countdown.

| 0:35 | Loop fires, "why" panel (35 s) |
| 1:10 | Coach plan and adherence (25 s) |
| 1:35 | Evidence and honesty page (15 s) |
| 1:50 | Roadmap and close (10 s) |
